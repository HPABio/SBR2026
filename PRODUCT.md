# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

SynBioReactor Summit serves a balanced mix of audiences with equal weight:

- **Early-stage synbio founders** seeking visibility, pitch opportunities, and investor connections
- **Investors and corporate partners** evaluating deal flow, sponsorship tiers, and strategic alignment
- **Researchers and industry experts** attending for content, panels, and credibility within the European synbio ecosystem

Visitors arrive with event intent: evaluating whether to attend, sponsor, partner, or pitch. They expect a credible, high-energy summit site—not a generic conference template. Internal surfaces (component-check, element-gallery) serve the build team but are secondary to the public marketing experience.

## Product Purpose

SynBioReactor Summit 2026 is Berlin's premier synthetic biology startup and investor summit. The site exists to:

1. **Convert sponsors and partners** — the top success metric
2. **Drive registrations and ticket sales**
3. **Build year-round community** beyond the single event
4. **Establish prestige** as the definitive European synbio gathering

The product is the event brand itself. Design is not in service of a separate app workflow; the interface *is* the pitch for why SBR2026 matters.

## Brand Personality

**Bold, scientific, future-forward.**

Voice is confident and direct—founder energy grounded in real science, not hype. The site should feel like a summit worth flying to Berlin for: high production value, intentional motion, and a dark canvas with saturated orange (#F49B2B) as the committed accent. Typography (Anton display, DM Sans body) and glow/border effects are part of the established identity and should evolve, not be replaced.

Emotional goals: excitement, credibility, urgency (limited seats, deadline-driven CTAs), and belonging to a cutting-edge community.

## Anti-references

Explicitly avoid:

- **Generic SaaS landing pages** — cream backgrounds, gradient text, identical icon-card grids, tiny uppercase eyebrows on every section
- **Corporate institutional dull** — safe gray palettes, forgettable layouts, no personality
- **Over-animated gimmicks** — bounce/elastic easing, parallax overload, motion that gates content visibility
- **Dry academic** — paper-white backgrounds, dense unbroken prose, no energy or visual hierarchy

Do not drift toward "AI conference site" tropes. The current dark + orange + glow direction is the anchor.

## Design Principles

1. **Design is the product** — Every public page is a brand statement. Sponsors and founders judge the summit by how the site feels before they read a word.
2. **Bold science, not hype** — Energy and motion serve credibility. Show real outcomes (speakers, partners, pitch winners) rather than abstract promises.
3. **Sponsors first, community always** — Surface sponsorship value and partner logos prominently; CTAs and copy should reflect the success priority (sponsors → registrations → community → prestige).
4. **Evolve the identity, don't reboot it** — Extend the existing dark theme, orange accent, Anton/Boldonse display, and glow components. Identity preservation wins over trend-chasing.
5. **Motion with purpose** — Animation enhances already-visible content. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. No bounce, no content gated behind reveal classes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target **WCAG 2.1 AA** for all public pages
- Pay special attention to **contrast on dark backgrounds** (orange primary, muted-foreground body text)
- All motion must have a **reduced-motion alternative** (crossfade or instant)
- Forms (registration, contact) must be keyboard-navigable with visible focus states
